# Planned user research

No participants have been recruited or interviewed.

Invite 3–5 people who actually create, buy or source digital assets. Include different asset specialisms and, when expanding the catalogue, contributors from the regions represented. Explain the prototype, ask consent before recording and publish no personal data without specific permission.

Before showing the design, ask about the participant's most recent real sourcing/listing experience, where they worked, which details mattered and what went wrong. Avoid “would you use my app?” questions.

Then observe three tasks: find a relevant asset; explain compatibility and the sample licence; prepare a sample creator listing. Record completion, confusion, misinterpretation of cultural context and mistaken expectations about real purchases/uploads. Do not invent completion rates or testimonials. Summarise evidence and contradictions before choosing changes.
