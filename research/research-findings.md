# Research findings and evidence status

**Date:** 29 October 2026. **Method:** project-material review, a limited public cultural-reference desk review and a marketplace reference review. **User participants:** none.

## Observations actually supported by the work

1. The supplied brand guide and kit define a proposed portfolio project, not completed product UI or user-research evidence. They propose dark colours, readable typography, clear metadata and simulated journeys.
2. The project owner selected dark premium styling and desktop with key mobile screens, then clarified that most assets should be rooted in African subjects. Those are design constraints, not user-study findings.
3. Museum, heritage and academic sources provide context for selected asset references. See [cultural references](cultural-references.md) for the individual sources and careful limits.
4. Reviewed CGTrader, Blendkit and Superhive public pages supplied by the project owner, plus selected public guidance/product details. Search, category structure, compatibility and creator attribution are documented as observable patterns in the [marketplace reference review](marketplace-reference-review.md). Their usability and business effectiveness were not tested.

## Design hypotheses still unvalidated

- Buyers sourcing African settings may benefit from region/context information alongside compatibility.
- A three-step listing process may make publishing feel manageable.
- A restrained dark UI may help evaluate bright asset previews.
- Creators may want a dedicated storefront for this catalogue focus.

No claim is made about market size, willingness to pay, competitor shortcomings, conversion, speed of use or customer preference.

## What has not happened

No interviews, usability studies, survey, ethnographic fieldwork, comprehensive competitive product audit or community consultation. The limited public-page reference review does not test actual buying or selling. There are no participant quotes or research-backed personas. Automated checks and a browser walkthrough are quality assurance, not target-user research.

## Next evidence to collect

Recruit 3–5 creators/buyers with informed consent. Ask about their most recent sourcing/listing experience before showing the concept. Then observe whether they can locate an asset, explain the example licence and complete the sample upload. Record errors, hesitation and contradictory feedback; avoid publishing identifying information. Use observations to revise the interface and update this file.
