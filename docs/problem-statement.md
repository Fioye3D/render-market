# Problem statement

## Product hypothesis

An asset marketplace centred on African digital creatives could make it easier to find and evaluate objects, materials and environments grounded in African places and cultures. The design explores clear discovery, useful technical metadata, cultural context and a guided listing workflow.

This is a hypothesis supplied by the project brief, not a verified market gap. No claim is made that African creators lack existing platforms or that a new marketplace is commercially viable.

## Intended users

- Independent 3D artists, material designers, animators and environment artists.
- Game developers, filmmakers and designers sourcing assets for African settings.
- Small creative studios evaluating compatibility and usage rights.

## Questions to validate

1. Where do creators and buyers currently find relevant digital assets?
2. Which asset types are difficult to source, and why?
3. What technical and contextual details influence a purchase decision?
4. How do creators describe the cultural references in their work?
5. What would justify listing on another platform?

## First design scope

Desktop marketplace, selected mobile buyer screens and two complete simulated journeys. Five of the six sample entries have African place/cultural references. The initial selection concentrates on West Africa and does not claim comprehensive African representation.

## Portfolio outcomes to assess

A reviewer should understand the concept, complete a buyer journey, complete a creator journey, inspect a consistent design system and distinguish the simulation from a real marketplace. These are evaluation goals, not measured user outcomes.
