# RenderMarket: Project story

RenderMarket began with my interest in Blender, 3D art and African creative environments. I wanted to explore how a marketplace could help people discover assets depicting familiar places, objects and everyday experiences. Created as an independent UI/UX portfolio project, RenderMarket explores asset discovery, creator profiles, interactive 3D previews and clear file information. It is now being developed as a product extension of Fioye Studios. The current website is a prototype. Research with potential buyers, production-ready catalogue development, authentication, payments and protected downloads remain part of the next phase.

This is an original project narrative. No competitor interviews, research results, personal experiences or market leadership claims are adopted.
