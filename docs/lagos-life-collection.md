# Everyday Lagos collection

Created for this portfolio iteration on 29 October 2026. These are fictional scene studies, not scans, commissioned projects or replicas of named businesses. They were authored with substantial Codex assistance using Blender scripts. The goal is a semi-realistic, economical catalogue treatment: metre-scale layouts, restrained edge radii, reusable 512px colour maps, readable objects and uncluttered scene composition.

The canteen, barber, chapel and classroom are deliberately open cutaways for inspection. Other scenes are open site compositions. This is stated in the listings rather than implying complete buildings.

## Geometry, textures and formats

Original editable Blender scenes retain separate objects, materials, cameras and lighting. GLB previews contain actual exported geometry and embedded image maps; the website does not substitute a rotating render for 3D inspection. OBJ is distributed with its MTL and supporting colour maps in a ZIP. FBX embeds its textures. BLEND sources pack image files.

The supplied maps are original small procedural colour textures generated by `tools/make_lagos_textures.py`. They are not full scanned PBR texture sets. Metalness and roughness use scalar material values. Browser lighting and mirror reflections can differ from the authored Cycles renders. No LOD system, rigging, character animation, simulation or production-game performance certification is claimed.

Measured export sizes, triangle counts, image presence and format checks are recorded in `design/lagos-asset-audit.json` after generation. Small files are a design objective; the report provides the measured result rather than an unsupported performance claim.

## Human geometry and credit

The occupied scenes use the MakeHuman project's neutral human base mesh, a bundled graphical asset released under CC0. This supplies the body geometry; it is not claimed as an original sculpt. Clothing surfaces, material assignment, headwrap/apron props and static pose transformations were scripted for this collection. Characters are fictional clothed adults and do not depict real people.

Source: [official MakeHuman base mesh](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj). The source mesh and original asset licence are preserved in `assets/vendor/makehuman/`. MakeHuman application code was not incorporated. See the [licence overview](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md) and [bundled-asset CC0 licence](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md).

## Marketplace presentation

Everyday Lagos is discoverable from the homepage and library. Searches include descriptions as well as titles and tags; “mama put”, “akara woman”, “church”, “football field” and “characters” resolve to the relevant listings. Each asset page states scene contents, character count, static-pose status and measured download sizes. Prices are sample UI content; downloads do not require payment, and checkout/payment services are not implemented.

Native Figma account import and public GitHub/hosting publication remain pending. Local reference artboards and the browser implementation are separate deliverables.
