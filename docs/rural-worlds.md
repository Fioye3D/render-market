# Rural Worlds

Twelve original procedural Blender environment studies, created with Codex assistance. They are fictional illustrative layouts, not photogrammetry, authenticated heritage reconstructions or production-ready realistic scans. Human base: credited MakeHuman CC0. Cattle and agricultural plants are procedural static geometry. Characters and clothing require manual refinement. No animation, animal rigging, crop simulation or water simulation is included.

Fulani herdsman is a respectful fictional adult character interpretation with cattle, not an ethnographic costume claim. The palace, mosque and shrine are fictional. The shrine scene depicts a plant-preparation space with an adult herbalist; it does not reproduce sacred symbols, assert medicinal efficacy or claim specific religious authenticity.

All twelve have real source BLEND, GLB, FBX and OBJ exports. Renders are optimized WebP previews. Prices remain proposed; paid delivery/authentication are not yet connected. Existing public assets are not secured by hiding storefront download links.

Homepage composition adopts CGTrader's search and category hierarchy, discovery shelves, collections and format browsing while using RenderMarket branding, navy/blue palette and the owner's UI exclusions. No copied CGTrader graphics, subscription claims, ratings or sales counts.
