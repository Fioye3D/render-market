## Everyday Lagos source asset

The neutral human body geometry is the official MakeHuman bundled `base.obj`, released under **CC0**, with source and original notices preserved in `assets/vendor/makehuman/`. It was downloaded from the [official repository](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj). Only the graphical asset was incorporated, not the AGPL application code. Clothing shells, poses, apron/headwrap props and scene layouts were scripted with Codex assistance. Body geometry is not claimed as an original sculpt. [Collection notes](lagos-life-collection.md) give scope, format and provenance details.
